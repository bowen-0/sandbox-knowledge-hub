# Swiss Implementation of the Council of Europe AI Convention

Switzerland's implementation of the Council of Europe's *Framework Convention on Artificial Intelligence and Human Rights, Democracy and the Rule of Law* — the first international legally-binding instrument specifically on AI, opened for signature 2024. The Swiss Federal Council's implementation decision came in early 2025; this is referenced in the booklet as the *Bundesratsentscheid zur KI-Regulierung* [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 21)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Relevance to the corpus

The booklet's §04 legal findings in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] state, under the heading *«KI bewegt sich nicht im rechtlichen Vakuum»* ("AI does not operate in a legal vacuum"):

> *«Bestehende gesetzliche Vorgaben – insbesondere Datenschutz und sektorspezifische Regulierung – sind in allen Projekten zentral und regeln bereits einen Grossteil der wichtigsten Fragen. KI-spezifische Regulierung, etwa im Zuge der Umsetzung der KI-Konvention in Schweizer Recht, erweitert diesen Rahmen gezielt, insbesondere in Bereichen, in denen KI-Systeme Grundrechte berühren.»*

In English: "Existing legal frameworks, e.g. in data protection and sector-specific regulation, are central in all projects and already address many of the key issues. AI-specific regulation, such as the implementation of the AI Convention into Swiss law, complements this framework in a targeted manner, especially in areas where AI systems affect fundamental rights." [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 18)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

This positions the Convention as the **fundamental-rights backstop** to sector-specific regulation rather than as a replacement for it. The framing matters for any system that processes data in ways that affect individual rights — which is the entire Phase II health-sector portfolio.

## Sandbox impact

The booklet ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §05) credits the sandbox with concrete input into the *Bundesratsentscheid zur KI-Regulierung*: sandbox findings on testing environments, cross-border collaboration, and sector-specific compliance fed into the federal-level decision [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 21)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The German records, under the bullet *«Policy-Impulse auf nationaler Ebene»* (policy impulses at the national level):

> *«Beiträge zur Weiterentwicklung von KI-Policy-Instrumenten, insbesondere im Kontext des Bundesratsentscheids zur KI-Regulierung, der Sandboxes als Instrument der Innovationsförderung verankerte, und als Grundlage für einen Vorstoss aus dem Ständerat zu KI-Testumgebungen.»*

In English: contributions to the further development of AI policy instruments, in particular in the context of the Federal Council decision on AI regulation, which anchored sandboxes as an instrument of innovation promotion, and as the basis for a motion from the Council of States on AI testing environments (translation ours) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 21)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
