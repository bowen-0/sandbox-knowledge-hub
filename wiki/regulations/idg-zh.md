# IDG ZH — Cantonal Information and Data Protection Act (Zurich)

The Canton of Zurich's *Informations- und Datenschutzgesetz* (IDG). Applies whenever a **public body in Canton Zurich** is the data controller — the cantonal counterpart to the federal [[dsg-fadp]] (which applies when a private provider is the controller). The building-permits legal chapter states it plainly: *"The IDG ZH applies in the canton of Zurich."* [(Building Permits report, p. 26)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md); the Phase I smart-parking analysis sets out the same controller-based split between cantonal and federal data-protection law [(Smart Parking report, p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md).

## Active revision noted in [[building-permits]]

The IDG ZH is **currently being revised** to add a transparency register for algorithmic decision-making systems with fundamental-rights impact, the *Verzeichnis für algorithmische Entscheidsysteme* (AES) [(Building Permits report, p. 24)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). The report on the revision: *"The legal basis for this is being created as part of the revision of the cantonal law on information and data protection (IDG ZH)."* [(Building Permits report, p. 24)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). [[building-permits]] flags this as load-bearing for future AI deployments in cantonal administrative procedures — the revised IDG ZH is likely to be the most relevant Swiss data-protection update for sandbox-aligned applications.
