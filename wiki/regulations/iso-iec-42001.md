# ISO/IEC 42001 — AI Management System Standard

The booklet glossary in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] defines the standard as follows:

> *«Internationale Norm für Managementsysteme im Bereich Künstliche Intelligenz. Sie definiert Anforderungen an Governance, Risikomanagement und organisatorische Prozesse für die verantwortungsvolle Entwicklung und Nutzung von KI.»*

In English: "An international standard for management systems in the field of artificial intelligence. It defines requirements for governance, risk management, and organisational processes to ensure the responsible development and use of AI." [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

ISO/IEC 42001 is the first international AI-management-system standard. Published in 2023, it sits structurally alongside ISO 9001 (quality management) and ISO 27001 (information security) but is specific to AI.

## Phase II usage in the sandbox

The standard was piloted concretely in [[inspection-robots]] — the project (in collaboration with [[modulos]]) tested the application of ISO/IEC 42001 on an exemplary basis as part of the autonomous-robot regulatory analysis [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). This makes ISO/IEC 42001 the **only AI-governance standard operationalised on a concrete Phase II use case**.
