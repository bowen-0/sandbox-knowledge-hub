# Swiss FADP / DSG — Federal Act on Data Protection

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP in English, *Datenschutzgesetz* / DSG in German). Revised version in force since September 2023. The dominant Swiss data-protection statute, applicable whenever a **private provider** is the data controller — paired with [[idg-zh]] when a **public body** is the data controller [(Smart Parking report, p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md).

## Key articles cited in the corpus

- **Art. 21 FADP** — Automated individual decisions; right to human review. Discussed in [[building-permits]], where the report concludes fully automated rejection of a building application would likely not be permissible under current law generally (*"Such fully automated decisions would likely not be permissible under current law."* — the report derives this from several higher-ranking legal bases, not from the FADP alone) [(Building Permits report, p. 26)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). Also referenced in [[machine-translation]] and Phase II healthcare projects. See [[fadp-art-21]] for the dedicated article page.

## Phase I usage

The booklet's "consistent Swiss legal stack" appears in every Phase I project:

1. **Cantonal IDG** ([[idg-zh]]) — when a public body controls the data.
2. **Federal FADP/DSG** (this regulation) — when a private provider controls the data.
3. **Commissioned-data-processing contracts** (*Auftragsdatenbearbeitung*) — for any third-party tool.

The Phase I smart-parking legal analysis states the first two layers verbatim: *"The Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) is applicable for data processing by private persons or by federal authorities. As for data processing by cantonal authorities, there are specific cantonal (information and) data protection laws; the applicable law in the Canton of Zurich, for instance, is the law governing information and data protection [Gesetz über die Information und den Datenschutz (IDG)]."* [(Smart Parking report, p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). The same page requires a written contract whenever a third party processes data on the controller's behalf, the *Auftragsdatenbearbeitung* layer [(Smart Parking report, p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md); [[ai-in-education]] applies the same frame to public schools [(AI in Education report, p. 7)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md).
