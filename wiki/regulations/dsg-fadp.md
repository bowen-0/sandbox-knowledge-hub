# Swiss FADP / DSG — Federal Act on Data Protection

The Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP in English, *Datenschutzgesetz* / DSG in German). Revised version in force since September 2023. The dominant Swiss data-protection statute, applicable whenever a **private provider** is the data controller — paired with [[idg-zh]] when a **public body** is the data controller [(p1-smart-parking p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md).

## Key articles cited in the corpus

- **Art. 21 FADP** — Automated individual decisions; right to human review. Cited in [[building-permits]] as the reason fully automated rejection of a building application would likely breach the FADP [(p2-building-permits p. 26)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). Also referenced in [[machine-translation]] and Phase II healthcare projects. See [[fadp-art-21]] for the dedicated article page.

## Phase I usage

The booklet's "consistent Swiss legal stack" appears in every Phase I project:

1. **Cantonal IDG** ([[idg-zh]]) — when a public body controls the data.
2. **Federal FADP/DSG** (this regulation) — when a private provider controls the data.
3. **Commissioned-data-processing contracts** (*Auftragsdatenbearbeitung*) — for any third-party tool.

The Phase I smart-parking legal analysis states the first two layers verbatim: *«Für Datenbearbeitungen durch Private oder durch Bundesbehörden kommt das Datenschutzrecht (DSG) zur Anwendung. Für Datenbearbeitungen durch kantonale Behörden gibt es spezielle kantonale (Informations- und) Datenschutzgesetze, im Kanton Zürich bspw. ist es das Informations- und Datenschutzgesetz (IDG).»* (processing by private actors or by federal authorities falls under the DSG; processing by cantonal authorities falls under special cantonal information and data-protection acts, in Canton Zurich the IDG) [(p1-smart-parking p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). The same page requires a written contract whenever a third party processes data on the controller's behalf, the *Auftragsdatenbearbeitung* layer [(p1-smart-parking p. 11)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md); [[ai-in-education]] applies the same frame to public schools [(p1-ai-in-education p. 7)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md).
