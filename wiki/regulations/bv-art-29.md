# BV Art. 29 — Procedural Guarantees and the Right to Be Heard

Article 29 of the Swiss Federal Constitution (*Bundesverfassung*, BV) guarantees general procedural rights before judicial and administrative authorities, including the right to be heard (*Anspruch auf rechtliches Gehör*). It is a constitutional provision rather than an ordinary statute; it is filed under `instrument: act` because the schema has no separate value for constitutional norms.

## Why it's load-bearing in the corpus

The [[building-permits]] legal analysis derives the duty to give reasons for administrative decisions from this article: *«Dazu gehört die Pflicht, eine Entscheidung zu begründen. Dies ergibt sich aus dem Anspruch auf rechtliches Gehör gemäss Art. 29 BV.»* (the duty to state reasons for a decision follows from the right to be heard under Art. 29 BV) [(p2-building-permits p. 25)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). From this the analysis draws an explainability requirement for any AI involvement: *«Wird eine Entscheidung unter Einsatz eines KI-Systems vorbereitet oder von einem solchen beeinflusst, müssen die zugrunde liegenden Logiken nachvollziehbar und in ihren wesentlichen Zügen erklärbar sein.»* (where a decision is prepared with or influenced by an AI system, the underlying logics must be comprehensible and explainable in their essential features); AI systems may therefore not deliver opaque black-box results that parties and appeal bodies cannot review [(p2-building-permits p. 25)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

## Paired with FADP Art. 21

Together with [[fadp-art-21]], Art. 29 BV completes the corpus's automated-decision framing. Cantonal law in Zurich contains no explicit norm granting human review of an AI-supported pre-check, but the building-permits analysis argues such a claim could derive indirectly from superordinate legal bases, naming the constitutional right to be heard, the rule-of-law principle and the general requirements of data-protection law: in sum, a duty of human review derived from federal and constitutional law [(p2-building-permits p. 26)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).
