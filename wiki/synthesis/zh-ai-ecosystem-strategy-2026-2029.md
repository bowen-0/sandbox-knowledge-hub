# Canton Zurich AI Ecosystem Strategy 2026–2029 — 4-Pillar Strategy

The strategic frame that the AI Innovation Sandbox now sits within. Following from Phase II, the **Government Council of the Canton of Zurich** decided to strengthen the AI ecosystem with a four-pillar approach for 2026–2029, operationalised through the *Amt für Wirtschaft* and partners [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). This is the most recent macro-context in the corpus: the audience for sandbox knowledge is no longer just "people running a pilot" but "people executing on one of four ecosystem pillars."

The booklet states the four-pillar approach directly: *"Building on the insights from the sandbox, the canton is pursuing an integrated approach along four pillars: the further development of the AI sandbox as a testing and learning environment, targeted support for AI start-ups, enabling SMEs to adopt and use AI, and the expansion of a structured AI dialogue with the public."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 25)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

Where the English edition's figure says "AI Ecosystem", the German original (Abbildung 3, p. 24) frames the strategy around the *KI-Standort* — Zurich as an AI *location* — and titles the decision *Stärkung des KI-Standorts* (strengthening the AI location) [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## What the strategy means for each pillar

1. **Pillar I — Sandbox.** Continues, but with refinements set out in §06 "Fazit und Ausblick": more agile project intake, differentiation of project types (regulatory analysis vs. real-world testing), thematic calls aligned to prioritised areas (e.g. the financial sector for Zurich), structured access to insights (thematic playbooks, one-stop shop, an AI-supported interaction component such as a chatbot), and public dialogue [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 23)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
2. **Pillar II — Start-up Support.** Physical AI hubs and scaling support [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
3. **Pillar III — SME Adoption.** Knowledge transfer and upskilling to strengthen AI capabilities at SMEs [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
4. **Pillar IV — Public Dialogue.** Already established in 10+ municipalities of the canton; being systematised and expanded [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 25)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Booklet's closing framing

> *"The AI sandbox thus marks not a conclusion, but the starting point of a long-term AI ecosystem strategy. It has demonstrated how AI can be responsibly tested, appropriately contextualised within regulatory frameworks, and made practically usable, thereby laying the foundation for the next phase of development of the AI ecosystem in Zurich through 2029."*
>
> [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 25)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)
