# Canton Zurich AI Ecosystem Strategy 2026–2029 — 4-Pillar Strategy

The strategic frame that the AI Innovation Sandbox now sits within. Following from Phase II, the **Government Council of the Canton of Zurich** decided to strengthen the AI ecosystem with a four-pillar approach for 2026–2029, operationalised through the *Amt für Wirtschaft* and partners [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). This is the most recent macro-context in the corpus: the audience for sandbox knowledge is no longer just "people running a pilot" but "people executing on one of four ecosystem pillars."

The booklet states the four-pillar approach directly: *«Aufbauend auf den Erkenntnissen aus der Sandbox verfolgt der Kanton einen integrierten Ansatz entlang von vier Säulen: der Weiterentwicklung der KI-Sandbox als Test- und Lernumgebung, der gezielten Förderung von KI-Start-ups, der Befähigung von KMU zur Nutzung von KI sowie des Ausbaus eines strukturierten KI-Dialogs mit der Bevölkerung.»* In English: building on the insights from the sandbox, the canton pursues an integrated approach along four pillars: further development of the AI sandbox as a testing and learning environment, targeted support for AI start-ups, enabling SMEs to use AI, and expansion of a structured AI dialogue with the public. [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 25)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In the German original (Abbildung 3, p. 24), the structure carries the title «KI-Standort», the decision bar reads *Regierungsratsbeschluss* «Stärkung des KI-Standorts», and the four pillars are «Innovation-Sandbox für KI», «KI-Startup-Förderung», «KI-Nutzung bei KMU», and «Bevölkerungsdialog» [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## What the strategy means for each pillar

1. **Pillar I — Sandbox.** Continues, but with refinements set out in §06 "Fazit und Ausblick": more agile project intake, differentiation of project types (regulatory analysis vs. real-world testing), thematic calls aligned to prioritised areas (e.g. the financial sector for Zurich), structured access to insights (thematic playbooks, one-stop shop, an AI-supported interaction component such as a chatbot), and public dialogue [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 23)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
2. **Pillar II — Start-up Support.** Physical AI hubs and scaling support [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
3. **Pillar III — SME Adoption.** Knowledge transfer and upskilling to strengthen AI capabilities at SMEs [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 24)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
4. **Pillar IV — Public Dialogue.** Already established in 10+ municipalities of the canton; being systematised and expanded [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 25)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Booklet's closing framing

> *«Damit markiert die KI-Sandbox nicht den Abschluss, sondern den Auftakt einer langfristigen KI-Standortstrategie. Sie hat gezeigt, wie KI verantwortungsvoll getestet, regulatorisch eingeordnet und praktisch nutzbar gemacht werden kann – und bildet damit die Grundlage für die nächste Entwicklungsstufe des KI-Standorts Zürich bis 2029.»*
>
> In the booklet's English edition: "The AI sandbox thus marks not a conclusion, but the starting point of a long-term AI ecosystem strategy. It has demonstrated how AI can be responsibly tested, appropriately contextualised within regulatory frameworks, and made practically usable, thereby laying the foundation for the next phase of development of the AI ecosystem in Zurich through 2029." (EN version, p. 25)
