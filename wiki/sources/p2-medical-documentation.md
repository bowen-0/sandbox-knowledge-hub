# AI in Medical Documentation — Legal Frameworks and Recommendations

**Read the report:** [PDF (DE)](../pdfs/de/p2-medical-documentation.pdf) · [PDF (EN)](../pdfs/en/p2-medical-documentation.pdf)

Phase II `analysis-only` project report. Legal foundations + practice-oriented guideline for AI-supported transcription, structuring, and improvement of medical documentation, anchored on the [[mpassist]] use case. Stringent data-protection requirements blocked operational deployment in a public hospital, so the project focused on the systematic clarification of regulatory issues. To scale results, the project organised a [[regulatory-roundtable]] with 10+ start-ups and the competent data-protection supervisory authority — an effective format because AI providers rarely have direct access to supervisory authorities. Active regulatory work with [[swissmedic]], Department of Health Canton ZH, and the eHealth working group of swissICT.

## Methodology

The report is pure regulatory analysis: no operational testing with patient data took place, so the findings rest on desk research plus structured expert consultation. The Office for Economy of Canton Zurich ([[amt-fuer-wirtschaft-zh]]) and the Center for Information Technology, Society, and Law ([[itsl-uzh]], University of Zurich) developed the legal foundations jointly within the Innovation Sandbox, involving partners from business, academia, and administration [(p2-medical-documentation p. 6)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md). The anchor case is [[mpassist]] (MPAssist AG), which submitted its project proposal to the sandbox in summer 2024; the report's content was developed against this concrete case [(p2-medical-documentation p. 44)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

The findings take the form of five artifacts:

- A **data-protection FAQ** for AI in medical documentation (chapter 2, pp. 7–18), condensed into a decision-tree figure covering data source, public vs private provider, DPIA, cloud processing, encryption options, US providers, and server location [(p2-medical-documentation p. 12)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- A **medical-device qualification framework** built on MepV/IvDV, EU MDR/IVDR, Swissmedic guidance, and MDCG 2019-11 (chapter 3) [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 19–21)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- **Eight worked use-case scenarios**, from dictation transcription to automated patient recall, each paired with a regulatory-assessment sidebar, closing with the MDR Annex VIII Rule 11 classification table (chapter 4) [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 22–35)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- **13 best practices** [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 36–38)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md) and **7 strategic ideas** for the future [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 39–40)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- An institutionalised **stakeholder dialogue**: the regulatory roundtable with 10+ start-ups and the cantonal data-protection supervisory authority, codified in the report as best practice 13, institutionalised round tables between stakeholders [(p2-medical-documentation p. 38)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

Ten named experts supported the work: Dr. André Baumgart (VZK, Verband Zürcher Krankenhäuser), Dr. Nadine Bienefeld (ETH Zürich), Michèle Hess, Raffaele Lugli, and Peter Waldner (all Gesundheitsdirektion Kanton Zürich), Dr. Rolf Kaufmann (Senior Medical Device Expert), Dr. med. Michael Neugebauer and Sebastian Svetel (both Universitäts-Kinderspital Zürich), Corinne Spirig (digital health center bülach), and Dr. Atanas Todorov (Arcondis) [(p2-medical-documentation p. 4)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

## Notable quotes

- *«LLMs passen in bestehende regulatorische Konzepte – vorausgesetzt die Funktionsabgrenzung ist klar und die Dokumentation sorgfältig.»* LLMs fit into existing regulatory concepts, provided the functional delineation is clear and the documentation careful. (Atanas Todorov, Chief Medical Officer, Arcondis, [p2-medical-documentation p. 34](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *«KI braucht eine Regulierung, die mitlernt – flexibel, risikobasiert und jenseits starrer Zulassungen.»* AI needs regulation that learns along: flexible, risk-based, and beyond rigid approvals. ([[raphael-von-thiessen]], Programme Lead AI Sandbox, Canton of Zurich, [p2-medical-documentation p. 40](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *«Die rechtssichere Anonymisierung von Medizinberichten ist praktisch kaum umsetzbar.»* Legally robust anonymisation of medical reports is barely feasible in practice. ([[stephanie-volz]], Managing Director ITSL, University of Zurich, [p2-medical-documentation p. 9](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *«Der Nutzen von KI in der Dokumentation ist gross, aber nur unter klaren Spielregeln für Datenschutz, Verantwortung und Transparenz.»* The benefit of AI in documentation is great, but only under clear ground rules for data protection, responsibility, and transparency. (Corinne Spirig, Chief Operating Officer, digital health center bülach, [p2-medical-documentation p. 16](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
