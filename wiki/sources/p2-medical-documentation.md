# AI in Medical Documentation — Legal Frameworks and Recommendations

**Read the report:** [PDF (DE)](../pdfs/de/p2-medical-documentation.pdf) · [PDF (EN)](../pdfs/en/p2-medical-documentation.pdf)

Phase II `analysis-only` project report. Legal foundations + practice-oriented guideline for AI-supported transcription, structuring, and improvement of medical documentation, anchored on the [[mpassist]] use case. Stringent data-protection requirements blocked operational deployment in a public hospital, so the project focused on the systematic clarification of regulatory issues. To scale results, the project organised a [[regulatory-roundtable]] with 10+ start-ups and the competent data-protection supervisory authority — an effective format because AI providers rarely have direct access to supervisory authorities. Active regulatory work with [[swissmedic]], Department of Health Canton ZH, and the eHealth working group of swissICT.

## Methodology

The report is pure regulatory analysis: no operational testing with patient data took place, so the findings rest on desk research plus structured expert consultation. The Office for Economy of Canton Zurich ([[amt-fuer-wirtschaft-zh]]) and the Center for Information Technology, Society, and Law ([[itsl-uzh]], University of Zurich) developed the legal foundations jointly within the Innovation Sandbox, involving partners from business, academia, and administration [(Medical Documentation report, p. 6)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md). The anchor case is [[mpassist]] (MPAssist AG), which submitted its project proposal to the sandbox in summer 2024; the report's content was developed against this concrete case [(Medical Documentation report, p. 42)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

The findings take the form of five artifacts:

- A **data-protection FAQ** for AI in medical documentation (chapter 2, pp. 7–17), condensed into a decision-tree figure covering data source, public vs private provider, DPIA, cloud processing, encryption options, US providers, and server location [(Medical Documentation report, p. 12)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- A **medical-device qualification framework** built on MedDO/IvDO, EU MDR/IVDR, Swissmedic guidance, and MDCG 2019-11 (chapter 3) [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 18–19)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- **Eight worked use-case scenarios**, from dictation transcription to automatic patient requests, each paired with a regulatory-assessment sidebar, including the MDR Annex VIII Rule 11 classification table (chapter 4) [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 20–33)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- **13 best practices** [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 34–36)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md) and **7 strategic ideas** for the future [(p2-medical-documentation pp. 37–38)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
- An institutionalised **stakeholder dialogue**: the regulatory roundtable with 10+ start-ups and the cantonal data-protection supervisory authority, codified in the report as best practice 13, institutionalised round tables between stakeholders [(Medical Documentation report, p. 36)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

Ten named experts supported the work: Dr. André Baumgart (VZK, Verband Zürcher Krankenhäuser), Dr. Nadine Bienefeld (ETH Zürich), Michèle Hess, Raffaele Lugli, and Peter Waldner (all Gesundheitsdirektion Kanton Zürich), Dr. Rolf Kaufmann (Senior Medical Device Expert), Dr. med. Michael Neugebauer and Sebastian Svetel (both Universitäts-Kinderspital Zürich), Corinne Spirig (digital health center bülach), and Dr. Atanas Todorov (Arcondis) [(Medical Documentation report, p. 4)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).

## Notable quotes

- *"LLMs fit into existing regulatory frameworks – provided their function is clearly defined and documentation is thorough."* (Atanas Todorov, Chief Medical Officer, Arcondis, [p2-medical-documentation p. 32](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *"AI needs new regulatory approaches – flexible, risk-based, and beyond rigid approvals."* ([[raphael-von-thiessen]], Programme Lead AI Sandbox, Canton of Zurich, [p2-medical-documentation p. 38](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *"Legally compliant anonymisation of medical reports is practically unfeasible."* ([[stephanie-volz]], Managing Director ITSL, University of Zurich, [p2-medical-documentation p. 9](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
- *"The benefits of AI in documentation are significant – but only if clear rules for data protection, responsibility, and transparency are in place."* (Corinne Spirig, Chief Operating Officer, digital health center bülach, [p2-medical-documentation p. 15](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md))
