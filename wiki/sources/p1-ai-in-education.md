# Artificial Intelligence in Education — Legal Best Practices

**Read the report:** [PDF (DE)](../pdfs/de/p1-ai-in-education.pdf) · [PDF (EN)](../pdfs/en/p1-ai-in-education.pdf)

Phase I project report. Legal best-practices framework for AI use in classroom / educational settings. Covers data-protection of minors, school-IT access (the operational bottleneck), and an early articulation of the privacy-by-design vocabulary (low resolution, masking, edge computing, instant deletion, data minimisation) that the Phase I corpus subsequently reuses as canonical.

## Provenance

## Methodology

The guide distils a single sandbox case into legal best practices. Herby Vision AG submitted a project proposal to the sandbox in spring 2022; its tool delivers automated correction of primary-school assignments by checking handwritten learning content with AI-based image recognition from a smartphone scan [(p1-ai-in-education p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Direct school access proved difficult to arrange; through the testbed programme of the Swiss EdTech Collider, Herby was able to test its offering at several schools [(p1-ai-in-education p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The guide's content was developed between July 2022 and September 2023 on the basis of this concrete application [(p1-ai-in-education p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The legal analysis rests on the legal bases of a public school in Canton Zürich; the legal situation in other cantons is comparable, though the provisions are applied differently. The guide addresses providers first and foremost, while also offering insights to school officials [(p1-ai-in-education p. 2)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Legal support during the sandbox led the start-up to move features dependent on personal data into the background and centre the product on pure automated correction, matching the report's data-minimising design guidance (no exact birth date at registration, guest access, QR-code allocation of learning content) [(p1-ai-in-education p. 8)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md).

Authors: [[stephanie-volz]] (legal expert of the sandbox, managing director [[itsl-uzh]]) and [[raphael-von-thiessen]] (head of the Innovation Sandbox for AI, Standortförderung Kanton Zürich) [(p1-ai-in-education p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Expert interviews and content collaboration: René Moser (Volksschulamt Kanton Zürich), Nelly Buchser and Karen Grossmann (Educa), Manuel Brogli (Kellerhals Carrard), Verena Rohrer and Carmen Sieber (Swiss EdTech Collider), and Moria Zürrer (school principal and president, Schule Medien Informatik Zürich) [(p1-ai-in-education p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The publishing Standortförderung sits within the [[amt-fuer-wirtschaft-zh]], the sandbox lead (impressum, unpaginated).

## Notable quotes

- *«KI-Projekte an Schulen sind aus rechtlicher Sicht vielschichtig, weshalb sich eine holistische Herangehensweise empfiehlt.»* — AI projects at schools are legally multilayered, which is why a holistic approach is recommended. [[stephanie-volz]], [[itsl-uzh]] ([p1-ai-in-education p. 4](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md))
- *«Das Anfallen von Personendaten, die für eine KI-Anwendung nicht zwingend benötigt werden, erhöht die rechtliche Komplexität massiv.»* — the accrual of personal data not strictly needed for an AI application massively increases legal complexity. [[stephanie-volz]], [[itsl-uzh]] ([p1-ai-in-education p. 8](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md))
- *«Die Einbindung von Large Language Models bringt für Hersteller Grosse Chancen, führt aber auch zu rechtlichen Risiken, die schwierig zu kontrollieren sind.»* — integrating large language models brings manufacturers great opportunities but also leads to legal risks that are difficult to control. [[raphael-von-thiessen]], head of the Innovation Sandbox for AI ([p1-ai-in-education p. 12](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md))
