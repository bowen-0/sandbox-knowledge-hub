# Artificial Intelligence in Education — Legal Best Practices

**Read the report:** [PDF (DE)](../pdfs/de/p1-ai-in-education.pdf) · [PDF (EN)](../pdfs/en/p1-ai-in-education.pdf)

Phase I project report. Legal best-practices framework for AI use in classroom / educational settings. Covers data-protection of minors, school-IT access (the operational bottleneck), and an early articulation of the privacy-by-design vocabulary (low resolution, masking, edge computing, instant deletion, data minimisation) that the Phase I corpus subsequently reuses as canonical.

## Provenance

Published October 2023. Original filename: `best_practices_ki_bildung_EN.pdf`. ## Methodology

The guide distils a single sandbox case into legal best practices. Herby Vision AG submitted a project proposal to the sandbox in spring 2022; its tool delivers automated correction of primary-school assignments by checking handwritten learning content with AI-based image recognition from a smartphone scan [(AI in Education report, p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Direct school access proved difficult to arrange; through the testbed programme of the Swiss EdTech Collider, Herby was able to test its offering at several schools [(AI in Education report, p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The guide's content was developed between July 2022 and September 2023 on the basis of this concrete application [(AI in Education report, p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The legal analysis rests on the legal bases of a public school in Canton Zürich; the legal situation in other cantons is comparable, though the provisions are applied differently. The guide addresses providers first and foremost, while also offering insights to school officials [(AI in Education report, p. 2)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Legal support during the sandbox led the start-up to move features dependent on personal data into the background and centre the product on pure automated correction, matching the report's data-minimising design guidance (no exact birth date at registration, guest access, QR-code allocation of learning content) [(AI in Education report, p. 8)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md).

Authors: [[stephanie-volz]] (legal expert of the sandbox, managing director [[itsl-uzh]]) and [[raphael-von-thiessen]] (head of the Innovation Sandbox for AI, Standortförderung Kanton Zürich) [(AI in Education report, p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). Expert interviews and content collaboration: René Moser (Volksschulamt Kanton Zürich), Nelly Buchser and Karen Grossmann (Educa), Manuel Brogli (Kellerhals Carrard), Verena Rohrer and Carmen Sieber (Swiss EdTech Collider), and Moria Zürrer (school principal and president, Schule Medien Informatik Zürich) [(AI in Education report, p. 14)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The publishing Standortförderung sits within the [[amt-fuer-wirtschaft-zh]], the sandbox lead (impressum, unpaginated).
