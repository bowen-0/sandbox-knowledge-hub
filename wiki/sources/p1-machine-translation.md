# Machine Translation — Recommendations for Public Administration

**Read the report:** [PDF (DE)](../pdfs/de/p1-machine-translation.pdf) · [PDF (EN)](../pdfs/en/p1-machine-translation.pdf)

Phase I project report. Recommendations for public-administration adoption of MT for official documents. Examines the Swiss legal stack (cantonal IDG / federal FADP / commissioned-data-processing) when administrative text data flows through a third-party MT service. Subsequent commercial-register-extracts MT solution was successfully rolled out, including scaling with Kanton Schwyz — cited in the Build & Share booklet ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]]) as a Phase I roll-out success.

## Methodology

The report builds on two parallel case studies. Two Swiss translation start-ups independently submitted AI projects to the sandbox between March and June 2022; because of the thematic overlap, the partners ran both cases simultaneously to illuminate different aspects of the same topic [(p1-machine-translation p. 10)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). Neur.on, a LegalTech start-up specialised in law, tax and banking [(p1-machine-translation p. 10)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md), built a specific translation model for the Commercial Register of Canton Schwyz, trained on around 20,000 register entries, starting German-to-English with French and Italian planned next; optimisation drew on expert input from the head of the commercial register and Neur.on specialists [(p1-machine-translation p. 11)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). Textshuttle, a University of Zurich spin-off, enriched its business solution for the Fachstelle Integration (integration unit) of Canton Zürich with human-translated administrative terminology in English, French, Italian and Polish [(p1-machine-translation p. 14)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md).

For the second case, professional translators blind-tested three translation variants of the «Willkommen im Kanton Zürich» web text: DeepL Pro, Textshuttle with stored administrative terminology (between 119 stored terms for Polish and 139 for English), and a certified human translation [(p1-machine-translation p. 15)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md), scored on six criteria: accuracy, fluency, terminology, grammar, completeness and cultural appropriateness [(p1-machine-translation p. 16)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). With three evaluations per language (twelve in total), the report itself frames the benchmarking as a practice-oriented check, not a scientific study, and states the results are not generalisable beyond the concrete use case [(p1-machine-translation p. 15)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). The two use cases were implemented between January and December 2023 [(p1-machine-translation p. 24)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md).

Authors: [[raphael-von-thiessen]] (head of the Innovation Sandbox for AI, Standortförderung Kanton Zürich) and [[stephanie-volz]] (legal expert of the sandbox, managing director [[itsl-uzh]]) [(p1-machine-translation p. 24)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). Expert interviews and content collaboration: Paula Reichenberg (Neur.on), Mara Bertamini (Textshuttle), Othmar Aeschi (Handelsregister Kanton Schwyz) and Nadia Sambuco (Fachstelle Integration Kanton Zürich) [(p1-machine-translation p. 24)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). ## Notable quotes

- *«Der Einsatz von maschineller Übersetzung muss zielgerichtet und differenziert erfolgen.»* — the use of machine translation must be targeted and differentiated. [[raphael-von-thiessen]], head of the Innovation Sandbox for AI ([p1-machine-translation p. 7](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md))
- *«Bei amtlichen Informationen birgt maschinelle Übersetzung Reputations- und Haftungsrisiken. Transparenz und klare Hinweise auf mögliche Fehler sind erforderlich.»* — for official information, machine translation carries reputational and liability risks; transparency and clear notices of possible errors are required. [[stephanie-volz]], [[itsl-uzh]] ([p1-machine-translation p. 8](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md))
