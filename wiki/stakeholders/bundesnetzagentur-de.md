# Bundesnetzagentur (Federal Network Agency, Germany)

Germany's federal network agency, one of the two non-Swiss authorities the Phase II [[inspection-robots]] project engaged, marking the sandbox programme's first cross-border collaboration with competent supervisory authorities. The booklet records: *«Erstmals arbeitete das Sandbox-Projekt zudem grenzüberschreitend mit zuständigen Aufsichtsbehörden zusammen – der Bundesnetzagentur in Deutschland und der Stabsstelle für Digitale Innovation des Fürstentums Liechtenstein.»* In English: for the first time, the sandbox project also worked across borders with competent supervisory authorities, the Federal Network Agency in Germany and the Office for Digital Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Role in the sandbox

The German project report lists Yvonne Finger, Referatsleiterin (head of unit) at the Bundesnetzagentur, among the experts who supported the project, under the heading *«Mit fachlicher Unterstützung durch»* (with expert support from) [(p2-inspection-robots p. 4)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md); the booklet's acknowledgements repeat the listing [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 27)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The corpus treats this engagement as the precedent for the cross-border pattern: a Swiss-led sandbox project gaining direct contact with an EU member-state authority on [[eu-ai-act]] and [[machinery-regulation]] classification questions. See [[cross-border-collab-unlocks-eu-access]] for the atomic lesson.
