# Bundesnetzagentur (Federal Network Agency, Germany)

Germany's federal network agency, one of the two non-Swiss authorities the Phase II [[inspection-robots]] project engaged, marking the sandbox programme's first cross-border collaboration with competent supervisory authorities. The booklet records: *"For the first time, the sandbox project also engaged in cross-border collaboration with competent supervisory authorities, e.g. the Federal Network Agency in Germany and the Office for Digital Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

## Role in the sandbox

The German project report lists Yvonne Finger, Referatsleiterin (head of unit) at the Bundesnetzagentur, among the experts who supported the project, under the heading *"With expert support from"* [(Inspection Robots report, p. 4)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md); the booklet's acknowledgements repeat the listing [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 27)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The corpus treats this engagement as the precedent for the cross-border pattern: a Swiss-led sandbox project gaining direct contact with an EU member-state authority on [[eu-ai-act]] and [[machinery-regulation]] classification questions. See [[cross-border-collab-unlocks-eu-access]] for the atomic lesson.
