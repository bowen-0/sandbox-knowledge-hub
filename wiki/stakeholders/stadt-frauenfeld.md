# Stadt Frauenfeld

The city of Frauenfeld was the test partner for [[smart-parking]], the Phase I pilot on camera-based parking-occupancy detection: *"In this AI Sandbox project, the city of Frauenfeld is testing the use of image recognition to improve public car park management."* [(Smart Parking report, p. 4)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). Parquery AG, the ETH spin-off providing the solution, submitted the proposal to the sandbox in spring 2022; the city then agreed to implement the concrete use case as partner. The city-side goal, in the words of Peter Koch, Stadt Frauenfeld: *"Our goal is to realise an efficient parking management based on intelligent technologies."* [(Smart Parking report, p. 5)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md).

## What the partnership contributed

Frauenfeld supplied the real-world operating environment: the city has 14 public car parks and parking garages in total, and the pilot covered 5 municipally owned car parks with roughly 500 spaces [(Smart Parking report, p. 5)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). With the city as project partner, the use case was implemented in practice between August 2022 and November 2023 [(Smart Parking report, p. 21)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md), and the resulting real-time occupancy data is integrated into the city's Regio app, with the Frauenfeld parking data also to become retrievable by car manufacturers' navigation apps via the Parkopedia interface [(Smart Parking report, p. 9)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). The report lists Peter Koch at the city's Amt für Kommunikation und Wirtschaftsförderung (office for communications and business development) among the contributing experts [(Smart Parking report, p. 21)](../sources/p1-smart-parking.md). The corpus cites this engagement as a canonical positive case of partner commitment, see [[strong-stakeholder-engagement-is-decisive]].
