# Gossweiler Ingenieure AG

Specialist partner ("Fachpartner") on the Phase II [[building-permits]] project. Because of the high domain complexity of the use case, the project team additionally worked with Gossweiler Ingenieure AG as Fachpartner [(Building Permits report, p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). The firm is represented in the project team by Richard König, Mitglied der Geschäftsleitung [(Building Permits report, p. 32)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

## Role in the sandbox

The report is explicit about why this partnership mattered: *"This partnership was crucial, as the ongoing exchange between the technological AI perspective and planning and approval practice and its specialist realities was a key prerequisite for achieving realistic results."* [(Building Permits report, p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) The `domain-sparring` value in the frontmatter maps this continuous, validation-oriented expert exchange onto the booklet's five-role taxonomy; the report itself uses the label "specialist partner" (*Fachpartner*).
