# Gossweiler Ingenieure AG

Specialist partner ("Fachpartner") on the Phase II [[building-permits]] project. Because of the high domain complexity of the use case, the project team additionally worked with Gossweiler Ingenieure AG as Fachpartner [(p2-building-permits p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). The firm is represented in the project team by Richard König, Mitglied der Geschäftsleitung [(p2-building-permits p. 32)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

## Role in the sandbox

The report is explicit about why this partnership mattered: *«Diese Partnerschaft war entscheidend, da der kontinuierliche Austausch zwischen der technologischen KI-Perspektive und der Planungs- und Bewilligungspraxis mit ihren fachlichen Gegebenheiten eine zentrale Voraussetzung für realistische Ergebnisse bildete.»* The partnership was decisive because continuous exchange between the technological AI perspective and planning-and-permitting practice, with its specialist realities, was a central precondition for realistic results [(p2-building-permits p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). The `domain-sparring` value in the frontmatter maps this continuous, validation-oriented expert exchange onto the booklet's five-role taxonomy; the report itself uses the label "Fachpartner".
