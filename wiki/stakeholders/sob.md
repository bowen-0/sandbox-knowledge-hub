# Schweizerische Südostbahn (SOB)

Swiss regional rail operator. Stepped in as the test partner for [[bridge-monitoring]] after the original municipal-footbridge partner withdrew — providing access to the **Reidholz railway bridge in Wädenswil** for sensor deployment under real train traffic.

## Role in the sandbox

The Phase II overview booklet, [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], describes the pivot: *"Following the withdrawal of the originally planned implementation partner for a municipal pedestrian bridge, the project was reoriented and carried out in collaboration with Schweizerische Südostbahn AG (SOB). At the Reidholz railway bridge in Wädenswil, sensors captured real loads from train traffic, providing for the first time a precise, data-based picture of the structural condition."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 13)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

This is the canonical Phase II instance of the **partner-pivot** pattern — see [[partner-bottleneck]] for the cross-cutting concept.
