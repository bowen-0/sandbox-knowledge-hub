# Byte Studio GmbH

Technical development partner on the Phase II [[building-permits]] project. The report names Byte Studio as one of the two project partners alongside [[nokema]], with the function "technischer Entwicklungspartner" (technical development partner) in the AI analysis run by the Amt für Wirtschaft of Canton Zürich [(Building Permits report, p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md), and as the project's "Technologiepartner" in the case-study summary [(Building Permits report, p. 32)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md). The firm is represented by its founders Noah Chavannes, Gründer und Software-Architekt, and Yves Rutishauser, Gründer und KI-Spezialist [(Building Permits report, p. 32)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

## Role in the sandbox

The report carries a pull quote from Rutishauser: *"In AI-based processing of building plans, the choice between LLM-based and rule-based approaches, as well as their continuous evaluation, is critical."* (Rutishauser, [p2-building-permits p. 13](../sources/p2-building-permits.md)). The prototype reflects that stance: procedure determination runs on deliberately non-generative if-then logic over the process matrix, with GIS data queried automatically via API [(Building Permits report, p. 20)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).
