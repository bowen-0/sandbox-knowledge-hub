# Stadt Kloten

City of Kloten, Canton of Zürich. Test partner for the Phase II [[building-permits]] project — provided **real building applications from the city** as training and evaluation data for the benchmark and prototype.

## Role in the sandbox

The Phase II overview booklet, [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], states the contribution directly: *"For model validation, the project used real building applications from the City of Kloten. Technical implementation was carried out in collaboration with the partners Nokema, Gossweiler Ingenieure, and Byte Studio."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 12)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

Stadt Kloten's contribution makes [[building-permits]] one of the few sandbox projects where AI evaluation was done on **real domain artefacts under expert ground-truth scoring** rather than on synthetic or generic test sets — the foundation of the [[llm-benchmarks]] methodology developed there [(Building Permits report, p. 9)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).
