# Data Protection Officer

The corpus uses the data protection officer (Datenschutzbeauftragte:r, DPO) as a generic function rather than a named stakeholder: the oversight role that reviews whether a data-processing project is data-protection compliant before it goes live. In Canton Zurich, public bodies must submit AI projects to the cantonal data protection officer for prior checking: *"In addition, if AI applications are used, the project must be submitted to the data protection officer for prior checking, as this involves new technologies that pose particular risks to the fundamental rights of the data subjects (Section 10(2) IDG in conjunction with Section 24 of the Ordinance on Data Protection and Information Security (IDV))."* [(Medical Documentation report, p. 11)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md). The education report describes what the prior check covers: *"Within the scope of prior checking, the data protection officers look into whether the envisaged project can be implemented in compliance with data protection requirements, or whether adjustments need to be made."* [(AI in Education report, p. 9)](../sources/p1-ai-in-education.md). The Zurich cantonal DPO has also published a guidance sheet (Merkblatt) on the procedure for AI use by cantonal and communal public bodies [(Medical Documentation report, p. 10)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md).
