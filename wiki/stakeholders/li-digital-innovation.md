# Office for Digital Innovation (Principality of Liechtenstein)

The Stabsstelle für Digitale Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein, one of the two non-Swiss authorities the Phase II [[inspection-robots]] project engaged in the sandbox programme's first cross-border collaboration. The booklet groups it with Germany's [[bundesnetzagentur-de]] as a competent supervisory authority: *"For the first time, the sandbox project also engaged in cross-border collaboration with competent supervisory authorities, e.g. the Federal Network Agency in Germany and the Office for Digital Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

## Role in the sandbox

The German project report lists Dr. Clara Guerra, Stabsstellenleiterin (head of the office), among the experts who supported the project, under the heading *"With expert support from"* [(Inspection Robots report, p. 4)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md); the booklet's acknowledgements repeat the listing [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 27)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). Together with the Bundesnetzagentur engagement, this gave the project an authority-side perspective beyond Switzerland on the [[eu-ai-act]] and [[machinery-regulation]] questions at its centre. See [[cross-border-collab-unlocks-eu-access]] for the atomic lesson.
