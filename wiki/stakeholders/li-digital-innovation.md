# Office for Digital Innovation (Principality of Liechtenstein)

The Stabsstelle für Digitale Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein, one of the two non-Swiss authorities the Phase II [[inspection-robots]] project engaged in the sandbox programme's first cross-border collaboration. The booklet groups it with Germany's [[bundesnetzagentur-de]] as a competent supervisory authority: *«Erstmals arbeitete das Sandbox-Projekt zudem grenzüberschreitend mit zuständigen Aufsichtsbehörden zusammen – der Bundesnetzagentur in Deutschland und der Stabsstelle für Digitale Innovation des Fürstentums Liechtenstein.»* In English: for the first time, the sandbox project also worked across borders with competent supervisory authorities, the Federal Network Agency in Germany and the Office for Digital Innovation of the Principality of Liechtenstein [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Role in the sandbox

The German project report lists Dr. Clara Guerra, Stabsstellenleiterin (head of the office), among the experts who supported the project, under the heading *«Mit fachlicher Unterstützung durch»* (with expert support from) [(p2-inspection-robots p. 4)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md); the booklet's acknowledgements repeat the listing [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 27)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). Together with the Bundesnetzagentur engagement, this gave the project an authority-side perspective beyond Switzerland on the [[eu-ai-act]] and [[machinery-regulation]] questions at its centre. See [[cross-border-collab-unlocks-eu-access]] for the atomic lesson.
