# Raphael von Thiessen

Programme Lead AI at the Canton of Zurich (Standortförderung, *Amt für Wirtschaft*). The **constant institutional anchor** of the sandbox across both phases — appears as project manager / programme lead on every sandbox report, both phase overviews, and is the named lead author of [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 27)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Role

- Strategic direction of the AI Innovation Sandbox programme.
- Co-author of the Phase I and Phase II final reports.
- Coordinates project portfolio, steering committee engagement, public communication [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 28)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Notable quotes in the corpus

> *«Unser Sandbox-Ansatz nutzt gezielt die Stärken des Schweizer KI-Ökosystems: Kollaboration, Offenheit und klarer Praxisfokus.»*
> — "Our sandbox approach deliberately leverages the strengths of the Swiss AI ecosystem: collaboration, openness, and a strong focus on practical application." (von Thiessen, [00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 4](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md))

> *«Die KI-Sandbox übersetzt konkrete Projekte systematisch in breitere Wirkung – von der Anwendung über den Wissenstransfer bis hin zur Gestaltung der Rahmenbedingungen.»*
> — "The AI sandbox systematically translates concrete projects into broader impact: from practical application and knowledge transfer to the shaping of AI policy." (von Thiessen, [00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 20](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md))
