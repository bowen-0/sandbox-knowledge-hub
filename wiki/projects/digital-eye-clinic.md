# Digital Eye Clinic

AI diagnostics for **diabetic retinopathy** screening in clinical practice. One of two Phase II healthcare projects (the other is [[medical-documentation]]), and the one that achieved real-world clinical testing [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 23)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md). Test partners: *Augenklinik Stadtspital Zürich* + *Spross Stiftung für Augenheilkunde* [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 2)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md).

## Outcome — three concrete deliverables

1. **A practice-tested evaluation framework** for AI diagnostic solutions, applicable beyond ophthalmology to other medical specialisations [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md), [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 14)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md).
2. **A modular AI diagnostic platform built on open-source technologies** — because no commercial provider met the stringent requirements of a public hospital [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md), [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 19)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md). The project explicitly demonstrated **intrapreneurship** and in-house development within the public healthcare sector: «Das Projekt zeigte damit das konkrete Potenzial von Intrapreneurship und Eigenentwicklungen im öffentlichen Gesundheitswesen.» [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)
3. **Regulatory, data-protection, and ethical best practices** for AI diagnostics in hospitals — transferable beyond the specific use case [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md), [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 26)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md).

## Key learnings

- Diabetic retinopathy is rising in patient volume; AI-enabled screening relieves medical staff and improves efficiency [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 2)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md).
- Open-source platforms can be a viable procurement alternative when commercial offerings cannot meet public-hospital requirements (data protection, transparency, modifiability) [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 29)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md). The report states that the team developed and validated its own AI models because no commercial provider met the clinic's key criteria: «Da kein kommerzieller Anbieter die wichtigsten Kriterien der Klinik erfüllte, entwickelte und validierte das Team eigene KI-Modelle auf der Basis von Open-Source-Forschungsframeworks.» [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 2)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md)
- The evaluation framework is reusable across medical AI deployments — one of the most transferable artefacts in Phase II [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 28)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md), [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
