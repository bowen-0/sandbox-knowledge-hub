# Autonomous Inspection Robots

`analysis-only` Phase II project — a structured regulatory pre-flight for **EU market access** of Swiss autonomous-robotics products. Anchored on the use case of [[anybotics]] (Swiss autonomous inspection robots in critical infrastructure) [(p2-inspection-robots p. 2)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md). No suitable test partner could be identified for operational deployment, so the project focused on **in-depth regulatory analysis** — which the sandbox treats as a successful outcome alongside [[medical-documentation]] [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 9)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The Build & Share booklet records the pivot verbatim: *«Da kein geeigneter Testpartner für einen operativen Einsatz identifiziert werden konnte, lag der Fokus auf einer vertieften regulatorischen Analyse»* (since no suitable test partner for operational use could be identified, the focus lay on an in-depth regulatory analysis) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Outcome

- A **structured regulatory classification** of autonomous inspection systems within the interplay of the **EU AI Act** and the **EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230** [(p2-inspection-robots p. 22)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md).
- Practice-oriented compliance strategies for Swiss vendors targeting the EU market [(p2-inspection-robots p. 7)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md).
- Tested an AI governance platform from [[modulos]] against the **ISO/IEC 42001** AI management-system standard [(p2-inspection-robots p. 29)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md).
- First-time **cross-border sandbox collaboration** with Germany's *Bundesnetzagentur* and Liechtenstein's *Office for Digital Innovation* [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- The interaction between the AI Act and the EU Machinery Regulation is dynamically evolving — single-instrument analysis is insufficient for multi-regulation AI products [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 14)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- ISO/IEC 42001 is concretely operationalisable on a real use case, not only as a paper standard [(p2-inspection-robots p. 35)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md).
- The "EU market access via Swiss sandbox" pattern is a viable model — relevant for the Phase III ecosystem strategy ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §05, "international networking" [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 21)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)). The report states the ambition directly: *«Die Erkenntnisse sollen weitere Robotikunternehmen unterstützen und den Zugang zum EU-Markt erleichtern.»* (the findings are meant to support other robotics companies and ease access to the EU market) [(p2-inspection-robots p. 2)](../sources/p2-inspection-robots.md).
