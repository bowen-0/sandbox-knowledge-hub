# Autonomous Systems

Regulatory guide for autonomous machinery — ground-contact autonomous systems such as autonomous tractors and agricultural systems, professional lawnmowers, cleaning robots and security robots; airborne, rail and waterborne systems are excluded — under the pre-EU-Machinery-Regulation Swiss legal framework [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 3)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md). The canonical Phase I source for the lesson [[integrate-regulation-early]] (**"integrate regulation early into product development"**). Retrofitting agricultural vehicles for automation required full re-certification of the product [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 13)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md), demonstrating that late-stage regulatory fixes drive flawed business models. The Phase II booklet states this directly: *"if regulatory requirements are addressed too late, flawed assumptions may arise in the business model"* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 18)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

## Outcome

Published regulatory guide. Cited prominently in [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Legal as the foundational evidence for the *regulate-early* pattern, naming the project's agricultural-vehicle re-certification example [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 18)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- Re-certification cost is the operative consequence of late-stage regulatory awareness. The guide is explicit for modified products: *"Accordingly, any certification will need to be reassessed."* [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 9)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md). For vehicles, retrofitting an autonomous component onto an approved vehicle triggers renewed examination and approval by ASTRA [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 13)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md).
- Predates the **EU Machinery Regulation 2023/1230** — which [[inspection-robots]] subsequently examines as the post-EU-revision update of this same domain. The guide's EU-level-developments chapter records the new machinery regulation, adopted 22 May 2023, as updating the 2006 machinery directive [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 20)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md).
- Swiss product-liability framing for autonomous machinery: product-liability act, employer liability, general fault-based and motor-vehicle liability [(Autonomous Systems report, p. 17)](../sources/p1-autonomous-systems.md).
