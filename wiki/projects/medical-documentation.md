# AI in Medical Documentation

A Phase II project that concluded as regulatory analysis without operational deployment. Legal foundations + practice-oriented guidelines for **AI-supported transcription, structuring, and improvement of medical documentation** [(Medical Documentation report, p. 2)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md), anchored on the [[mpassist]] use case [(Medical Documentation report, p. 42)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md). Stringent Swiss data-protection requirements blocked operational deployment of patient-data processing in a public hospital, so the project focused on systematic regulatory clarification — paralleling [[inspection-robots]] as a successful analysis-only outcome [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 9)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The booklet states the blocker plainly: *"Due to the stringent data protection requirements governing data processing, operational deployment with patient data in a public hospital was not feasible."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

## Outcome — the regulatory roundtable as a knowledge-transfer format

The project's most reusable artefact is not a prototype but a **format**: a **[[regulatory-roundtable|regulatory roundtable]]** with 10+ start-ups in the AI-supported medical-documentation space, convened together with the competent data-protection supervisory authority [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The booklet flags why the format worked: *"This format proved particularly effective, as AI providers rarely have direct access to supervisory authorities."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) Sustained regulatory work with **Swissmedic**, the **Department of Health Canton Zürich**, and the **eHealth working group of swissICT** [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- AI-supported medical documentation has both Large-Language-Model and operational dimensions; legal analysis must span data-protection + sectoral healthcare regulation simultaneously. [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Legal states the general principle: *"Interdisciplinary legal issues are the norm"* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 18)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- Regulatory roundtables are a high-leverage transfer format — bringing supervisory authorities and providers into direct exchange beyond bilateral case-by-case advice [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- The non-implementation of operational testing did not eliminate impact: *"The recommendations developed in the project quickly established themselves as a reference within the healthcare system"* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
