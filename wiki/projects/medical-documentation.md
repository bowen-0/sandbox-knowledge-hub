# AI in Medical Documentation

`analysis-only` Phase II project. Legal foundations + practice-oriented guidelines for **AI-supported transcription, structuring, and improvement of medical documentation** [(p2-medical-documentation p. 2)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md), anchored on the [[mpassist]] use case [(p2-medical-documentation p. 44)](../sources/p2-medical-documentation.md). Stringent Swiss data-protection requirements blocked operational deployment of patient-data processing in a public hospital, so the project focused on systematic regulatory clarification — paralleling [[inspection-robots]] as a successful analysis-only outcome [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 9)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The booklet states the blocker plainly: «Aufgrund der hohen datenschutzrechtlichen Anforderungen an die Datenverarbeitung konnte kein operativer Einsatz mit Patientendaten in einem Spital mit öffentlichen Leistungsauftrag erfolgen.» — owing to the high data-protection requirements on data processing, no operational use with patient data was possible in a hospital with a public service mandate [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Outcome — the regulatory roundtable as a knowledge-transfer format

The project's most reusable artefact is not a prototype but a **format**: a **[[regulatory-roundtable|regulatory roundtable]]** with 10+ start-ups in the AI-supported medical-documentation space, convened together with the competent data-protection supervisory authority [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The booklet flags this as particularly effective because AI providers rarely have direct access to supervisory authorities: «Dieses Format erwies sich als besonders wirkungsvoll, da KI-Anbieter selten direkten Zugang zu Aufsichtsbehörden erhalten.» [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) Sustained regulatory work with **Swissmedic**, the **Department of Health Canton Zürich**, and the **eHealth working group of swissICT** [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- AI-supported medical documentation has both Large-Language-Model and operational dimensions; legal analysis must span data-protection + sectoral healthcare regulation simultaneously. [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §04 Legal states the general principle: «Interdisziplinäre Rechtsfragen sind die Regel» — interdisciplinary legal issues are the norm [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 18)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- Regulatory roundtables are a high-leverage transfer format — bringing supervisory authorities and providers into direct exchange beyond bilateral case-by-case advice [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- The non-implementation of operational testing did not eliminate impact: «Die im Projekt erarbeiteten Empfehlungen etablierten sich rasch als Referenz im Gesundheitssystem» — the recommendations developed in the project quickly established themselves as a reference within the healthcare system [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 10)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
