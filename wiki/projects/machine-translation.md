# Machine Translation

Recommendations for public-administration adoption of machine translation for official documents [(p1-machine-translation p. 2)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md) [(p1-machine-translation p. 19)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md). Examines the **confidentiality and security requirements** that apply when administrative text flows through a third-party MT service: translated information typically leaves the administration's servers, so documents classified «vertraulich» or «geheim» may only be machine-translated via internal or certified external solutions (e.g. ISO 27001), with reputation, liability and transparency duties on top [(p1-machine-translation p. 8)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md).

## Outcome

The downstream **commercial-register-extracts MT solution** was, after a successful test with **Kanton Schwyz** (the Phase I case partner), rolled out to several further cantons; [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §01 cites it as a Phase I roll-out success alongside [[smart-parking]]. The booklet records: *«Die maschinelle Übersetzung für Handelsregisterauszüge wurde nach dem erfolgreichen Test mit dem Kanton Schwyz auf mehrere Kantone ausgerollt.»* (machine translation for commercial-register extracts was, after the successful test with Kanton Schwyz, rolled out to several cantons) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 5)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- Established that **third-party machine translation** of confidential or secret administrative documents comes into question only under strict conditions: the solution runs on internal servers, or the external solution is certified (e.g. ISO 27001), guarantees full transparency on data flows and cybersecurity, and is audited and approved by the public body concerned. The report states: *«Für Dokumente, die vertrauliche oder geheime Informationen enthalten, kommt eine maschinelle Übersetzung ausschliesslich in Betracht, wenn die Lösung auf den internen Servern installiert wird oder wenn die betreffende externe Lösung zertifiziert ist (z. B. nach ISO 27001), eine volle Transparenz bezüglich Datenflüsse und Cybersicherheit gewährleistet und die betroffene staatliche Stelle die Lösung auditiert und bewilligt.»* (for documents containing confidential or secret information, machine translation comes into consideration exclusively if the solution is installed on internal servers, or if the external solution in question is certified, e.g. to ISO 27001, guarantees full transparency on data flows and cybersecurity, and the public body concerned audits and approves the solution) [(p1-machine-translation p. 8)](../sources/p1-machine-translation.md).
- Linked in the corpus to the FADP Art. 21 / Constitution Art. 29 framing for automated administrative decisions ([[fadp-art-21]], [[bv-art-29]]); that framing is articulated in the [[building-permits]] legal analysis [(p2-building-permits p. 25)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) [(p2-building-permits p. 26)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).
- Demonstrates the cross-cantonal scaling pathway for sandbox outputs: tested with Kanton Schwyz, then rolled out to several further cantons [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 5)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
