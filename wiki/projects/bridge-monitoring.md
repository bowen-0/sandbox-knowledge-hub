# Sensor-based Bridge Monitoring

Sensor data + AI predictions for infrastructure maintenance. Originally planned with a municipal-footbridge partner; after that partner withdrew, the project was carried out at the **Reidholz railway bridge in Wädenswil** [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 13)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) in partnership with *Schweizerische Südostbahn AG* (SOB) [(p2-bridge-monitoring p. 8)](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md).

## Outcome

Sensors captured **real loads from train traffic for the first time**, providing a precise data-based picture of the bridge's structural condition; the booklet records it verbatim: «An der Eisenbahnbrücke Reidholz in Wädenswil erfassten Sensoren reale Belastungen aus dem Zugverkehr und lieferten erstmals ein präzises, datenbasiertes Bild des strukturellen Zustands.» [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 13)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The project then developed scenarios for the bridge's remaining service life, supporting concrete maintenance and investment decisions [(p2-bridge-monitoring p. 11)](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md). Demonstrates how data-driven approaches make critical-infrastructure monitoring safer, more efficient, and more sustainable — particularly in the heavily-utilised Metropolitan Area of Zurich [(p2-bridge-monitoring p. 2)](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 13)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Key learnings

- **Data-driven complements standards-based**: AI-supported monitoring does not replace human structural-engineering expertise — the booklet repeats this framing across multiple Phase II projects [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 19)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The report itself puts it as: «Datenbasiertes Monitoring ersetzt bestehende Nachweise nicht, sondern ergänzt sie mit Messdaten aus dem Betrieb.» Data-driven monitoring does not replace existing structural verification, it complements it with measurement data from live operations (von Thiessen, [p2-bridge-monitoring p. 7](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md)).
- **Partner withdrawal → pivot** is a legitimate sandbox execution pattern: the project preserved value by rerouting to a different partner rather than aborting [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 13)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
- Liability and data-protection regime for sensor-based monitoring systems was clarified [(p2-bridge-monitoring p. 13)](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md) [(p2-bridge-monitoring p. 14)](../sources/p2-bridge-monitoring.md) — direct legal carry-over from [[infrastructure-maintenance]] (Phase I) but with a different physical modality (sensor vs. drone imagery).
