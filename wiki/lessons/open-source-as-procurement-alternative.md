# Open-source AI is a viable procurement alternative when commercial doesn't fit

The booklet states the lesson directly in its Digital Eye Clinic summary ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03):

> *«Zweitens entwickelte das Projektteam auf Basis von Open Source eine eigene, modulare KI-Diagnostikplattform, da kein kommerzieller Anbieter die strengen Anforderungen eines öffentlichen Spitals erfüllte. Das Projekt zeigte damit das konkrete Potenzial von Intrapreneurship und Eigenentwicklungen im öffentlichen Gesundheitswesen.»*

In English: the project team built its own modular AI diagnostics platform on an open-source basis, as no commercial provider met the stringent requirements of a public hospital, demonstrating the tangible potential of [[intrapreneurship]] and in-house development within public healthcare [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

## Evidence base

- **[[digital-eye-clinic]]** — built an open-source modular AI diagnostic platform after surveying commercial providers and finding none that met public-hospital requirements [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 18)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md) (data protection, transparency, modifiability [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 20)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md)).
- **[[building-permits]]** — the working prototype's design favours **model-agnostic** integration [(p2-building-permits p. 13)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) and open-source-friendly architecture [(p2-building-permits p. 30)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md); this is partially driven by [[embag]] obligations on administrative software [(p2-building-permits p. 24)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

## How to apply

When evaluating commercial vs. open-source AI procurement, weigh:

1. **Data-protection fit** — can the commercial vendor process the data under your jurisdiction's regime? (Often a hard no in healthcare.)
2. **Transparency requirements** — can you audit what the system does? (Critical for administrative-law contexts; see [[fadp-art-21]].)
3. **Modifiability** — can you adapt the system as the use case evolves? (Critical for emerging or specialised domains.)
4. **EMBAG obligations** — if you're a Swiss public administration, [[embag]] presumes open-source disclosure of administrative software [(p2-building-permits p. 24)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).

Open-source is not a default; it's a tool that wins under specific constraints. The [[digital-eye-clinic]] decision was forced, not preferred [(p2-digital-eye-clinic p. 19)](../sources/p2-digital-eye-clinic.md).
