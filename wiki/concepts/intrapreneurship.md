# Intrapreneurship

> *"Entrepreneurial activity within established organisations, where employees initiate and drive innovation projects, often with an exploratory focus."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

A glossary term the booklet introduces specifically because of [[digital-eye-clinic]] (booklet §03): *"In doing so, the project demonstrated the tangible potential of intrapreneurship and in-house development within the public healthcare sector."* [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) The project had built a modular open-source AI diagnostic platform when no commercial provider met public-hospital requirements.

The pattern is operationally significant: when an organisation cannot procure a fit-for-purpose AI solution, building one in-house (with sandbox support) is a viable third path between commercial procurement and abandonment.
