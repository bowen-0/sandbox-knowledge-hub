# Intrapreneurship

> *«Unternehmerisches Handeln innerhalb bestehender Organisationen, bei dem Mitarbeitende Innovationsprojekte initiieren und vorantreiben, häufig mit explorativem Charakter.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: entrepreneurial activity within established organisations, where employees initiate and drive innovation projects, often with an exploratory focus.

A glossary term the booklet introduces specifically because of [[digital-eye-clinic]] (booklet §03): *«Das Projekt zeigte damit das konkrete Potenzial von Intrapreneurship und Eigenentwicklungen im öffentlichen Gesundheitswesen.»* The project demonstrated the tangible potential of intrapreneurship and in-house development within the public healthcare sector, by building a modular open-source AI diagnostic platform when no commercial provider met public-hospital requirements [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 11)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

The pattern is operationally significant: when an organisation cannot procure a fit-for-purpose AI solution, building one in-house (with sandbox support) is a viable third path between commercial procurement and abandonment.
