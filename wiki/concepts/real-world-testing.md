# Real-World Testing

> *«Erprobung eines KI-Systems unter realen Einsatzbedingungen mit echten Daten, Prozessen und organisatorischer Einbettung, um technische, regulatorische und operative Fragestellungen praxisnah zu validieren.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: the evaluation of an AI system under real operating conditions using actual data, processes, and organisational contexts, in order to validate technical, regulatory, and operational aspects in a practical setting.

The defining methodological commitment of the sandbox. The booklet's opening contrast (§01):

> *«Im Unterschied zu vielen internationalen Ansätzen liegt der Fokus auf Real-World-Testing statt auf rein regulatorischen Überprüfungen.»*
> — [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 4)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: in contrast to many international approaches, the focus lies on real-world testing rather than purely regulatory assessments.

**Three of five Phase II projects achieved real-world testing**: [[bridge-monitoring]], [[digital-eye-clinic]], [[building-permits]]. The other two ([[medical-documentation]], [[inspection-robots]]) pivoted to pure regulatory analysis — a legitimate sandbox outcome but a distinct project class [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 9)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md). The wiki distinguishes them via the `status: completed | analysis-only` frontmatter on project pages.

> [!tension] Real-world testing vs. analysis-only
> The sandbox treats both as successful outputs, but they answer different questions. Real-world testing produces operational evidence (deployment patterns, cost data, integration findings). Analysis-only projects produce regulatory clarity (compliance pathways, supervisory-authority engagement). When citing a Phase II lesson, check which kind of evidence supports it.
