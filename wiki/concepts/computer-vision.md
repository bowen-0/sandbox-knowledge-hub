# Computer Vision

> *«Teilgebiet der Künstlichen Intelligenz, das visuelle Daten (z. B. Bilder oder Videos) automatisiert analysiert und interpretiert, etwa zur Objekterkennung, Klassifikation oder Schadensdetektion.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: a subfield of artificial intelligence that automatically analyses and interprets visual data (e.g. images or videos), for example for object detection, classification, or damage identification.

The dominant technical paradigm of **Phase I** (2022–2024) — three of five Phase I pilots were computer-vision use cases ([[smart-parking]], [[infrastructure-maintenance]], partially [[autonomous-systems]]). Phase II shifted the centre of gravity toward [[generative-ai]] [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 5)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) but kept CV alive in [[bridge-monitoring]] (sensor + image fusion).

The Build & Share booklet flags CV as one of the high-impact non-LLM use case families in public-sector AI (§04 Technology, «KI ist mehr als LLMs», rendered "AI extends beyond large language models" in the EN edition) [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 17)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md); see the lesson [[ai-extends-beyond-large-language-models]].
