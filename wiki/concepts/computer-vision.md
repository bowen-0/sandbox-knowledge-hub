# Computer Vision

> *"A subfield of artificial intelligence that automatically analyses and interprets visual data (e.g. images or videos), for example for object detection, classification, or damage identification."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

The dominant technical paradigm of **Phase I** (2022–2024) — three of five Phase I pilots were computer-vision use cases ([[smart-parking]], [[infrastructure-maintenance]], partially [[autonomous-systems]]). Phase II shifted the centre of gravity toward [[generative-ai]] [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 5)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md) but kept CV alive in [[bridge-monitoring]] (sensor + image fusion).

The Build & Share booklet flags CV as one of the high-impact non-LLM use case families in public-sector AI (§04 Technology, "AI extends beyond large language models") [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 17)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md); see the lesson [[ai-extends-beyond-large-language-models]].
