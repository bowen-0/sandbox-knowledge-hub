# Deepfakes

> *«Mittels KI erzeugte oder manipulierte Audio-, Bild- oder Videoinhalte, die real wirkende, aber künstlich generierte Darstellungen von Personen oder Ereignissen erzeugen.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: audio, image, or video content generated or manipulated using AI to create realistic but artificially produced representations of individuals or events.

Appears in the Phase II corpus as a **submitted-but-discontinued** project. Deepfake detection in the context of identity fraud was one of three Phase II projects the sandbox team did not pursue further by mutual agreement, *«Drei davon verfolgte es im gegenseitigen Einvernehmen nicht weiter.»* ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03, «Weitere Projekte» / Additional Projects, [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)). The discontinuation reasons differ from the analysis-only outcomes of [[medical-documentation]] and [[inspection-robots]]. The booklet reports the reasons collectively: for two of the three discontinued projects no suitable implementation partners for operational testing were found, and in one case the research orientation and required financial contribution did not fit the sandbox's objectives. It does not specify which reason applied to deepfake detection [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
