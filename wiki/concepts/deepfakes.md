# Deepfakes

> *«Mittels KI erzeugte oder manipulierte Audio-, Bild- oder Videoinhalte, die real wirkende, aber künstlich generierte Darstellungen von Personen oder Ereignissen erzeugen.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: audio, image, or video content generated or manipulated using AI to create realistic but artificially produced representations of individuals or events.

Appears in the Phase II corpus as a **submitted-but-discontinued** project. Deepfake detection in the context of identity fraud was one of three Phase II projects the sandbox team did not pursue further by mutual agreement, *«Drei davon verfolgte es im gegenseitigen Einvernehmen nicht weiter.»* ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03, «Weitere Projekte» / Additional Projects, [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)). The discontinuation reasons differ from the analysis-only outcomes of [[medical-documentation]] and [[inspection-robots]]: deepfake detection was not implementable in the sandbox format primarily for partner-availability reasons [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).
