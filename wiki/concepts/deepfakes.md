# Deepfakes

> *"Audio, image, or video content generated or manipulated using AI to create realistic but artificially produced representations of individuals or events."*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

Appears in the Phase II corpus as a **submitted-but-discontinued** project. Deepfake detection in the context of identity fraud was one of three Phase II projects the sandbox team did not pursue further by mutual agreement: *"In Phase II, the sandbox team initiated a total of eight projects. Three of these were not pursued further by mutual agreement."* ([[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]] §03, Additional Projects, [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)). The discontinuation reasons differ from the analysis-only outcomes of [[medical-documentation]] and [[inspection-robots]]. The booklet reports the reasons collectively: for two of the three discontinued projects no suitable implementation partners for operational testing were found, and in one case the research orientation and required financial contribution did not fit the sandbox's objectives. It does not specify which reason applied to deepfake detection [(Build & Share (Phase II overview report), p. 15)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

> [!gap] No executed pilot
> No sandbox report exists for this concept. The page captures the booklet's framing but the corpus has no concrete operational evidence.
