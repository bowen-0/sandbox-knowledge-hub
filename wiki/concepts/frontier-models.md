# Frontier Models

> *«Sehr leistungsfähige, grosse KI-Modelle (z. B. grosse Sprach- oder Multimodalmodelle), die den aktuellen Stand der Technik repräsentieren. Sie werden mit umfangreichen Daten und Rechenressourcen trainiert und bilden die Grundlage vieler generativer KI-Anwendungen, bringen jedoch auch erhöhte Anforderungen an Sicherheit, Governance und Regulierung mit sich.»*
> — [[00-overview-phase2-build-and-share]], glossary [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 26)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md)

In English: highly advanced, large-scale AI models (e.g. large language or multimodal models) representing the current state of the art. They are trained on extensive datasets using substantial computational resources and underpin many generative AI applications, while also introducing heightened requirements for safety, governance, and regulation.

The Build & Share booklet (§04 Technology, «Frontier-Modelle vs. Open Source gezielt kombinieren», rendered "Combine frontier models and open-source solutions strategically" in the EN edition) makes the *combination* of frontier models + open-source models a strategic recommendation rather than a binary choice — requirements vary across data-protection, performance, cost, and scalability [(00-overview-phase2-build-and-share p. 17)](../sources/00-overview-phase2-build-and-share.md).

The single most rigorous test of frontier-model behaviour in the corpus is [[building-permits]]: 8 frontier models benchmarked [(p2-building-permits p. 10)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) across 3,336 evaluations [(p2-building-permits p. 11)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) using [[llm-as-a-judge]]. Headline finding: **no single model wins** [(p2-building-permits p. 14)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md) — model-agnostic architecture is mandatory [(p2-building-permits p. 13)](../sources/p2-building-permits.md).
